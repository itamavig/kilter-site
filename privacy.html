Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026 · Slant Puzzle for iOS

Slant Puzzle is published by Itamar Avigdori. This policy explains what the app stores, what it does not, and which third parties receive data when you play.

1. The app has no account and no server

There is no sign-up, no login, and no profile. Slant Puzzle sends nothing to us. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and we cannot see your progress, your purchases, or your device. The app contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

2. What is stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, on your phone only. It is not transmitted to us and is not backed up to any service we control:

  • Which levels you have completed, your star ratings, and how many hints and resets you used
  • Your coin balance and which purchases have been credited to you
  • Your progress through the tutorial
  • Counters used to pace how often ads appear, and the time your current session began
  • Whether ads have been switched off for you

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no other copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on request and no way for us to restore it.

3. Advertising, and the advertising identifier

Slant Puzzle shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob is a third party and collects data directly — this is the one place where information about you leaves your device. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), device and operating-system information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with the ads you are shown.

On first launch the app asks, through Apple's standard App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites:

  • If you allow it, AdMob may use your advertising identifier and you may be shown personalised ads.
  • If you decline, the identifier is not made available for tracking. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using it.

You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your device. What AdMob does with what it collects is governed by Google's own policy, not this one: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Purchases

Coin packs are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Your payment is handled entirely by Apple and we never see your card details, your Apple Account, or your name. The app receives only a confirmation that a purchase completed, which it uses to credit your coins. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Children

Slant Puzzle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The app is not configured as a child-directed service for advertising purposes, which means ads are served under Google's standard terms rather than its under-age-of-consent terms. If you are a parent or guardian and believe this is the wrong setting for your child's use of the app, please contact us.

6. Your choices

  • Turn off personalised ads — decline the tracking prompt, or revoke it later in iOS Settings, as described in section 3.
  • Delete everything — delete the app. Section 2 applies.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes to what is collected will be reflected here before the app version making that change is released.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in the app: slantpuzzle@icloud.com.
