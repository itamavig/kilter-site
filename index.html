Slant Puzzle

Support & contact

Slant is a logic puzzle. Fill every square with a diagonal line so that each numbered circle touches exactly that many lines, and no loop ever closes. Every puzzle in the app has exactly one solution.

Get in touch

Email slantpuzzle@icloud.com. One person reads it, so please allow a few days for a reply.

It helps enormously if you include your device model, your iOS version, and the level number you were on.

Common questions

I paid for coins and they haven't arrived

Closing the app completely and reopening it is worth trying first. If the coins are still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and roughly what time it was, and we will look into it. Apple handles refunds directly, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone and my progress is gone

Progress is stored on the device itself, not in an account, so it does not follow you to a new phone. This is also why we cannot restore it for you — there is no copy on our side. See the privacy policy for what is stored and where.

Can I turn off personalised ads?

Yes. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your device and turn off tracking for Slant Puzzle. Ads will still appear, but will not be personalised.

I'm stuck on a level

Every puzzle in the app has exactly one solution, so there is always a single correct answer to find. If you are stuck, the hint button will fill in a square for you.

I think I found a bug

Please email it. The level number and a screenshot are usually enough to reproduce it.
